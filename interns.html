Industry & Policy Research

  • U.S. & Japan medical-representative research. Traced how the U.S. (1937–2025) and Japan (1960–2025) regulated pharmaceutical sales representatives, including 41 coded U.S. enforcement cases. U.S. oversight widened from individual kickbacks to entire sales systems and then to the wider payment network, while Japan’s MR workforce shrank by a third from its FY2013 peak as headcount-driven coverage gave way to specialist roles.
  • China’s 2026 medical-representative rules, benchmarked. Mapped China’s Measures for the Administration of Medical Representatives (effective Aug 1, 2026) against seven Japanese mechanisms. China is compressing about 30 years of Japanese reform—separating promotion from sales and holding marketing-authorization holders accountable—into one regulation, though Japan’s experience suggests funding ties tend to resurface in new forms.
  • China medical-device VBP landscape. Built a source-linked database of China’s medical-device volume-based procurement (VBP) projects and policy changes through July 2026, condensed into a 20-milestone roadmap in Chinese and English. It shows a shift from “lowest price wins” toward stable supply and quality: price cuts narrowed from ~93% in the first coronary-stent round to milder results under the 6th batch’s anti-low-price floor.

Compensation & Incentive Consulting

  • Medical-device sales-incentive survey. Prepared and checked data for Aon’s China medical-device sales-incentive survey reports, including a six-company mini-survey, and analyzed plan practices across seven design levers: eligibility, plan type, sales and behavioral KPIs, frequency, target amount, pay mix, and payout curve, threshold and cap. The survey showed VBP-driven margin pressure pushing companies toward profit-focused incentives (margin metrics in sales plans, high-margin product focus for reps, cost-control goals for managers) and toward quarterly reviews and payouts.
  • Incentive redesign after VBP. Compared incentive plans before and after VBP: annual targets and share-grabbing commissions or hospital-entry bonuses are giving way to targets rebalanced across provinces, extra rewards for winning bids, recalibrated payout curves and thresholds, and KPIs for new channels, markets, customers and products. Drafted a shared-incentive structure for VBP-specialist roles (market access, VBP enablement, tendering) and a quarterly target-reset scheme for regions hit by procurement.
  • Growth incentive options. Drafted plan options for new markets (territory specialists vs. subdivided territories; local-hire vs. expatriate overseas teams), new customers (five customer definitions, per-hospital bonuses, weighted bonus modules, win-back multipliers), new products and trade-in upgrades, and redeploying unspent bonus pools when full-year targets are at risk. Each option was benchmarked against Aon’s 2026 medical-device sales-incentive survey.
  • R&D pay-competitiveness diagnostic. Analyzed and checked a compensation diagnostic for the R&D organization of a China-listed biopharmaceutical company: compa-ratios against the market median by grade and unit, pay mix and tenure cohorts. It showed where the company-wide average hid under-market pockets; findings were presented to client leadership.
